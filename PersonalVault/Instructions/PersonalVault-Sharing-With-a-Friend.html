Nothing of yours ever leaves your PC

Sharing Personal Vault with a friend

You send them the app. Everything else — their master secret, their Google Drive connection, their one-time-link setup if they want it — is entirely self-service on their end, with their own accounts. Your credentials.json, your vault, and your master secret never leave this PC.

What to send / what to keep private

✓ Send this

  • The zipped publish\ folder (the app itself)

✕ Keep this private

  • Your credentials.json
  • Your vault.pvlt / data folder
  • Your master secret
1
Command prompt → PersonalVault project folder

Build a portable copy

dotnet publish -c Release -r win-x64 --self-contained true -p:PublishSingleFile=true -p:IncludeNativeLibrariesForSelfExtract=true -o publish

Produces a publish\ folder with PersonalVault.exe and everything it needs bundled in (roughly 100-150 MB — that's the .NET runtime, which is why your friend needs no separate install).

2
Zip & send

Zip the publish folder and send it

Email, USB drive, shared folder — however you'd send any file. Double-check the zip only contains the publish\ folder, not your own %AppData% or data folder.

⚠️ Do not include credentials.json in this zip. That file lets whoever has it sign in to Drive as the app — each person should create their own, entirely free, in about ten minutes (Step 4 below covers it, fully self-service).
3
Their PC

They run it and create their own vault

  1. Unzip the folder anywhere — no install, no admin rights needed.
  2. Double-click PersonalVault.exe.
  3. Choose a master secret: minimum 12 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, and a symbol. There's no password reset — losing it means losing the vault.
  4. Add their first account.
✓ Their data lives in a PersonalVault folder created automatically right next to their copy of PersonalVault.exe — fully portable, and completely separate from yours even though it came from the same zip.
4
Optional

Google Drive backup — entirely self-service

Skip this if neither of you wants cloud backup — the app works fully offline. If they want it, they follow PersonalVault-Drive-Setup-Guide.html (in this same folder) start to finish: their own Google Cloud project, their own credentials.json, their own separate Drive file. Nothing from you is needed at any point.

5
Optional

One-time Share links — also entirely self-service

Unrelated to Drive backup — this is the Share... button that hands someone a real one-time link to a single password. Skip it and everything else still works normally. If they want it, they follow PersonalVault-Share-Setup-Guide.html (also in this folder): their own GitHub Pages site and their own Google Apps Script deployment.
